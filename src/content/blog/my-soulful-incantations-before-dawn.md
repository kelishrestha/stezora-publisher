When the house is holding its breath
and your name still feels like a question,
speak this low. It is older than the night.

---

Let the dark not have the last verb.
Let the tired hands find their blessing
before the light is asked to justify them.

Let what is broken in you
learn the quiet grammar of repair —
the way a wound is a word
the body keeps repeating
until it is heard.

Let the door you are afraid of
be a door and not a verdict.

And when morning comes —
for it does come,
even to the reluctant —
let it find you
not undefeated, but still beginning.

So it is spoken.
So the day may begin.
