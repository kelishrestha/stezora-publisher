For every yes you gave
that was really a *please*,
and every *please*
that was really a love letter,
this one is yours.

---

Take the unanswered yes
and let it be a garden instead of a question.
Some things are planted
precisely because they will not bloom on schedule.

Take the want you have apologized for
and hold it the way you hold a candle
to a window —
not to be seen, but to keep the dark
from getting the whole house.

You are not too much.
You were never too much.
You were a yes
still learning its own voice.

Return it to yourself, gently,
the way water returns to water.

It is yours.
It was always yours.
