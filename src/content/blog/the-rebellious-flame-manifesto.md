You were not made to be comfortable. You were made to be tended — and the world confuses tending with taming.

Every age produces its own cages, and ours is the cage of optimization. We are asked to manage ourselves the way one manages an asset: measurable outputs, steady growth, acceptable risk. We are asked to moderate the fire into a hearth. Somewhere along the way, we agreed.

## The fire does not negotiate

This is the rebellion: to refuse the small life on the grounds that it is small. Not loudly, necessarily. Rebellion in a single person often looks like rest. Like a no said without a paragraph of justification. Like work chosen for its meaning rather than its margin.

The flame inside you does not recognize the spreadsheet. It does not recognize the algorithm, the status, the carefully balanced portfolio of identities. It recognizes only one command, and it is not *become manageable*. It is *burn*.

## Tending is not taming

Tending a fire means feeding it, standing close enough to feel it, and accepting that it will occasionally scorch what you thought you needed. It means understanding that a small, obedient flame is not safety. It is a pilot light where a bonfire was promised.

So let this be the record of the refusal: we will not be managed. We will be tended — by ourselves, for ourselves, and for whatever comes next that is worth the heat.
