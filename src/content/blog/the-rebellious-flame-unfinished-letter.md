Every "good girl" and every "responsible man" was once a child who learned too well the art of being easy to keep.

Dear former self,

You were praised for being easy. For the predictable hours, the agreeable answers, the capacity to absorb other people's moods without comment. I know now that praise was a receipt, not a reward. You were paying for the privilege of taking up less space.

## The tax on compliance

Compliance has a compounding cost, and it is paid in appetite. In the things you stopped wanting because wanting them caused friction. In the opinions you filed away for a future that never came to claim them. Obedience, I have learned, is not a virtue. It is a strategy — useful sometimes, poisonous when it becomes the whole of your character.

## Unlearning is a burning

To unlearn is not to forget. It is to take the lesson that was bent around you and bend it back, and the heat of that work is what this publication is for.

So here, in this letter, I return what was borrowed: the approval, the ease, the smallness. I keep only the part of me that refuses to be kept. It was never lost. It was merely — forgive me — well-mannered.
